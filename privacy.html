Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We process TikTok Open ID, display name, avatar, and OAuth tokens solely to publish media and display analytics. We do NOT collect account passwords.

2. How We Use Information

Data is used exclusively to provide authorized publishing features and maintain system security.

3. Data Protection

Credentials and tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) at rest. We never sell, lease, or trade personal data to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Tokens can be deleted at any time by disconnecting the account in settings or revoking access on TikTok.

5. Third-Party Services

This service interacts with TikTok APIs under TikTok's Privacy Policy.
